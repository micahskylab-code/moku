Is that address in a tsunami evacuation zone?

Hawaii's official tsunami evacuation zones cover coastal land on every island. Moku shows the evacuation status of any parcel — your home, a rental you're booking, a property you're pricing — free, from the state's own maps.

Two kinds of zones

  • Tsunami evacuation zone — leave when any Tsunami Warning is issued.
  • Extreme evacuation zone — a larger area used for the rarest, largest events.

Outside both means the parcel sits beyond the state's mapped evacuation areas. Every parcel card on Moku shows this line automatically, and the map layer paints the zones across whole coastlines so you can see exactly where they start and stop.

When people check this

  • Before booking a coastal vacation rental — where would we go, and how far?
  • Before buying near the shore — insurance and peace of mind both read this map
  • During tsunami watches — know your zone before the sirens, not after

Evacuation zones are published by the State of Hawaii. In any real event, follow Civil Defense instructions — this map is for planning, not emergencies.
