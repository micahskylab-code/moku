About Moku

Moku is a free lookup of Hawaii’s official property records, built on Kauai by Micah Hanano, a local who wanted this to exist and builds it in the open. No accounts, no ads, nothing for sale on this site.

Where the data comes from: the State of Hawaii Statewide GIS Program, the counties’ own public map servers (parcels, tax rolls, the vacation‑rental registry, recorded sales where a county publishes them), FEMA flood zones via the state, and Realtor.com inventory via the St. Louis Fed. Records appear exactly as the government publishes them — verify with the county before relying on them for any transaction.

Privacy, in plain words: no tracking, no analytics cookies, no ad pixels. Your searches go straight to government map servers. If you tap “What’s under me?”, your location is used on your device and never stored or sent to us. If you write to Micah, your email is used only to reply.
