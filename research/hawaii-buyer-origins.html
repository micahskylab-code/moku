Market intelligence for Hawaiʻi brokers

One in four Hawaiʻi homes sells to someone who lives somewhere else.

On Hawaiʻi Island it is two in five. About a third of those out-of-state buyers are Californian, and most of the rest come from a short list of Western metros. This report shows who they are, why they buy, where they land, and how to reach them within fair-housing and ad-platform rules.

§1 · The island split

The neighbor islands are where outside money concentrates

Oʻahu has nearly two-thirds of all sales but the lowest outside share. Hawaiʻi Island, Kauaʻi and Maui each sell a third or more of their homes to buyers with addresses elsewhere.

Maui's outside share fell from about 35% to about 28% after the Lahaina fire and the Bill 9 vacation-rental phase-out. Oʻahu's rose. Outside demand is moving, not shrinking.
  • Condos are the outsider product. In 2024 out-of-state buyers took 31% of condo sales statewide, and more than half of neighbor-island condo sales, compared with 20% of single-family sales. Official UHERO Housing Factbook 2025.
  • Outside buyers bring more money. In 2024 the average mainland purchase was $1.21M and the average foreign purchase $1.64M, against $824K for local buyers. On Maui (H1 2025), mainland buyers were 29% of sales but 43% of dollar volume. Official / Reported
  • The highest-share ZIPs. Kōloa–Poʻipū (96756) and Princeville (96722) were each about 60% out-of-state buyers in 2024. Official UHERO 2025.
§2 · Where they come from in the U.S.

California first by a wide margin, then the Pacific Northwest

Title Guaranty tallies buyers by home state for each island. On both Hawaiʻi Island and Oʻahu, California alone accounts for about a third of buyers from outside the state. Washington is a steady second or third everywhere.

The other two islands

  • Kauaʻi: California leads every period: 112 buyers and $215M in 2024, and 51 buyers ($101M) in H1 2025. Colorado, Washington, Texas, Utah and Nevada follow at 12–14 each per half-year. Official / Reported
  • Maui: in H1 2025, California had 53 buyers ($130.5M) and Washington 21. Canada was the largest foreign source at $45.8M. Island Sotheby's describes Maui luxury buyers as mostly Seattle and Bay Area. Reported

Getting from states to metros

No official source reports buyer origin by city. Four independent signals all point to the same metros:

  • Where people search from. Redfin's top metros for users looking to move to Honolulu (Jan–Mar 2026) were Los Angeles (579 users), San Francisco (535) and Seattle (238). Reported
  • Where movers come from. In 2023, 58,539 people moved to Hawaiʻi from other states: California 11,200 (19%), Washington 4,092, Texas 3,742, Virginia 3,388 and Colorado 3,359. Official ACS via DBEDT Table 1.62.
  • Where repeat visitors come from. California sent 2.88M visitors in 2024, and Los Angeles alone was 9.1% of all arrivals. 82% of US West visitors had been before. Repeat visitors are the buyer pipeline. Official DBEDT 2024 Visitor Research.
  • Where nonstop flights come from. New 2026 routes widen the pipeline: Minneapolis, Chicago and Boston to Maui and Honolulu; Burbank, Ontario, Boise and Spokane to Honolulu; Salt Lake City to Kona; Las Vegas to Hilo. Reported
§3 · Where they come from abroad

Japan buys Oʻahu condos, Canada buys Maui and Kona, Korea is rising

Foreign buyers made only 2.8% of 2024 sales, but at an average $1.64M they are the highest-value segment. Their sales concentrate on very few islands and products.

§5 · Why they buy

Eight buyer types, each with its own trigger

"Hawaiʻi buyer" is several different people. Each type below has its own origin geography, island preference and timing, and needs its own message.

Largest outside segment

The West Coast second-home buyer

Affluent 45–64-year-olds who have vacationed here for years. Nationally, 85% of 2025 vacation-home mortgages went to high earners, with a median income of $294K Reported Redfin. Many buy with cash.

From
LA and Orange County, SF Bay Area, Seattle, Portland
Buys
Neighbor-island resort condos and homes
Reach
Mainland winter (Oct–Feb), while they are on island; retarget after visits
Tax-driven

The retiree

Hawaiʻi exempts Social Security and employer-funded pensions, government and military pensions included. IRA and 401(k) withdrawals are taxed. A new 13% top bracket from 2027 (from $1M joint income) removes any tax edge over California for the very wealthy Official

From
California public-pension retirees, retired military and federal workers
Buys

Reach

Coming home

The returning kamaʻāina

More Native Hawaiians now live on the mainland (about 370K) than in Hawaiʻi (about 309K). Young island-born residents are moving back. 65% of the 2021 remote-worker cohort were former residents Official

Being reshaped by law

The rental investor

Maui's Bill 9 ends short-term rental use for about 6,200–7,000 apartment-zoned units by 2029 (West Maui) and 2031 (elsewhere). Maui taxes long-term rentals at $2.90 per $1,000 against $13–17 for vacation rentals Official Legal resort-zone and VDA units command a premium.

§7 · Rules of the road

ZIP-level precision lives in the mail, not the ad platforms

Housing ads are a regulated category. The rules change which channel can carry which level of targeting.

Meta (FB/IG)
Ads must be declared under the Housing special category. There is no ZIP targeting, and a city or pin expands to at least a 15-mile radius. Age must stay 18–65+ with all genders, and lookalikes and exclusions are off. Allowed: origin cities and states (Seattle + 15 mi, all of California) and your own uploaded client list.
Google
Housing policy (US and Canada): no targeting by ZIP, age, gender, parental or marital status. Use city, metro, state or radius instead.
Fair housing
Targeting by place is fine when it adds reach. Targeting by country, language or ancestry can read as a national-origin preference under 42 USC 3604(c), and Hawaiʻi's HRS §515-3 adds ancestry, age and marital status as protected classes. Have Japan, Korea and Canada campaigns reviewed, and keep ad copy about the property, never about who the buyer should be.
Direct mail
The precise channel. Owner mailing addresses from county tax rolls are public records (OIP Op. Ltr. 11-01), and mail can go to any ZIP. Fair housing and brokerage-disclosure rules still apply to the mailer.
Every ad
HAR §16-99-11: show the brokerage's legal or registered trade name and the licensee's status. Team names must be registered trade names.
Phone, text, email
TCPA requires prior express written consent for autodialed or prerecorded marketing, a Do Not Call scrub every 31 days, and calls only 8am–9pm in the recipient's local time. CAN-SPAM requires a postal address and an opt-out honored within 10 business days.
Lahaina
The governor's proclamation bans unsolicited offers to burn-zone owners, with penalties of up to 1 year and $5,000. Do not prospect there. Reach sellers only through local and land-trust channels.

§8 · Getting to ZIP level

The next build: count every out-of-state owner by home ZIP

The published data stops at the state. The county tax rolls go further: every parcel carries the owner's mailing address of record. Counting those addresses gives an origin map down to the ZIP code, for each island and neighborhood. No published report has this.

  1. Maui County, available now. The county publishes a free "Property Owners & Mailing Addresses" extract on its Download Public Information page.
  2. Hawaiʻi County. qPublic search results export to CSV/XLSX, including mailing labels. Request a full roll from cohrpt@hawaiicounty.gov.
  3. Kauaʻi County. Mailing addresses are shown per parcel. Request a bulk roll under UIPA.
  4. Honolulu. The open-data tables (OWNERDAT, OWNALL) may not include mailing addresses. Moku already holds a UIPA sales extract from the city, so a second UIPA request for the owner-address roll fits the existing relationship (bfsrpmailbox@honolulu.gov).
  5. Join the rolls to Moku's parcels by TMK. That gives out-of-state owner counts by origin ZIP × island × neighborhood, weighted by assessed value, with tax class (homeowner exemption vs. non-owner-occupied) as a check.
  6. Verify every Official figure above against the source files: DBEDT Table 21.39, the Title Guaranty quarterly Buyer Stats PDFs (statewide, Q4 2025 and Q2 2026), and IRS SOI county-to-county inflows for 2022–23. The IRS data gives the top origin counties for each Hawaiʻi county, which is the official metro detail this report lacks.
