6 · Rules of the road

Target cities, not ZIP codes, and keep it fair

Meta and Google
Housing ads cannot target ZIP codes, age or gender. Use a city or metro with a radius (Meta: at least 15 miles in the U.S. and Canada, 15 km elsewhere), or your own client list if the platform marks it eligible.
Fair housing
No ad may signal a preference by national origin, race or any protected class (42 USC 3604(c); HRS §515-3), and targeting by place can act as a proxy for these. Keep geographic targeting broad and based on documented demand, have country-level or language-specific campaigns reviewed, and keep ad copy about the property, never the buyer.
Direct mail
Can go to any ZIP except 96761, 96767 and 96790 (see Lahaina and Kula). Owner mailing addresses in county tax records are public (OIP Op. Ltr. 11-01). Fair-housing and brokerage-name rules still apply.
Every ad
Show the brokerage's legal or registered trade name and the licensee's status (HAR §16-99-11).
Referrals
Referral fees to out-of-state agents go through licensed brokerages, under each state's rules.
Lahaina and Kula
The governor's wildfire proclamations ban unsolicited offers to buy any real property in ZIP codes 96761 and 96767 (Lahaina–West Maui) and 96790 (Kula). A violation is a misdemeanor. The current proclamation (August 24, 2026) runs to about October 23, 2026; check for a renewal. Do not prospect there.

A summary of rules, not legal advice. Confirm campaigns with your principal broker.
