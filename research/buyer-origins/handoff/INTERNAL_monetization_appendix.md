## What gives the product value

**Distinctive (hard to copy):**
- Buyer origin by island and Title Guaranty district.
- Off-island ownership aggregates from the county rolls.
- 19 years of IRS household flows.
- HMDA occupancy by neighborhood.
- District scorecards.
- Evidence-checked "why" explanations.
- All of it in one place, refreshed on each publisher's schedule.

**Easy to substitute** (mostly free elsewhere): Title Guaranty PDFs, the UHERO Factbook, DBEDT tables, RPR, and the board reports. Any paid value has to come from synthesis, timeliness and usable formats, not from raw access.

**Main business risks:**
- A small market: about 14,000 licensees and about a dozen sizeable brokerages.
- A new vendor with no track record.
- Data-licensing scope: Title Guaranty, the boards, Realtor.com and UHERO.
- RESPA and fair-housing exposure if lenders or title companies sponsor it.

## Business-model options (hypotheses, not offers)

| Option | Who pays | Price hypothesis from the archived plan | What would validate it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free public report + newsletter (now) | Nobody | — | Returning users per release; newsletter sign-ups and open rate; time on micro-market and district pages |
| Agent seat (monthly district brief, presentation slides) | Individual agents | About $39/month, month to month | 3+ months of repeat use by the same users; agents asking for specific districts; a paid test converting at least a few percent of active free users |
| Brokerage license (quarterly reports, their sales compared with the market, white-label public edition) | Brokerages | About $36K–60K/year; Enterprise with seats for all agents about $72K–108K | A pilot with written success metrics (listing appointments, use in presentations, recruiting) met by the end of the pilot |
| 90-day pilot | Brokerages | About $9K–15K, credited to an annual license | A signed pilot; results review; conversion to annual |
| Custom studies | Developers, lenders, appraisers | From about $7.5K per study | Inbound requests; repeat buyers |
| Data exports / research license (CSV + data dictionary for tables nobody else publishes) | Brokerage research teams, analysts | One-off or annual (not priced) | Requests from research teams; acceptable licensing terms for each source |

**Buyer's-eye review** (three simulated buyer personas, 2026-10-09; full output in this session):
- A brokerage owner and a Maui agent would each **start with a pilot or one paid month**.
- A rival research director would **not buy** as the report stood. That reviewer's credibility problems (process notes, inconsistent figures, nationality wording) have since been fixed in the report.
- Top requests:
  - Area-specific sample deliverables, such as a Maui condo kit and a Kohala Coast slide set.
  - Clear pricing by brokerage size.
  - A "your sales vs the market" view.
  - A neighbor-island luxury page built from public data.
  - A named team and references ("who we are").

## Prerequisites before charging

1. **Counsel memo** covering:
   - RESPA Section 8 structure: no lender, title or escrow money toward a brokerage's subscription, and no referral-based pricing.
   - Fair-housing copy.
   - TCPA and Do-Not-Call rules.
   - HRS 467 (referral fees; whether selling data requires a license).
   - Each county's terms for reusing owner data.
2. **Written data permissions:**
   - Title Guaranty: received, per Micah. Confirm its scope.
   - REALTOR boards and Fidelity: for republished statistics.
   - Realtor.com and UHERO: commercial use.
3. **Validation metrics agreed in advance:** repeat use and retention for the free product first; pilot metrics for any paid test.
4. **No owner-roll prospecting products** (mail lists and similar) without counsel review. Keep the Lahaina and Kula exclusion permanent.
