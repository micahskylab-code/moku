The one-line pitch

Most of the 2025 slowdown came from out-of-state buyers, and they are the buyers who pay the most. No other Hawaiʻi report shows, ZIP by ZIP, where those buyers come from, what they buy and where sellers go next. This one does.

  • About 48% of brokerages spend more than $100 per agent per month on technology.31
  • A 2023 CFPB order valued a data subscription at about $300 per agent per month.32

At 300 agents, Tier 1 works out to $10–$17 per agent per month Derived, which is below a single Altos seat. Price by brokerage rather than by seat: published head counts for Hawaii Life range from 275+ agents and staff to 335 agents.3,6

Why Hawaii Life first

It is the right size, its business is concentrated on the Neighbor Islands and in luxury, where out-of-state buyers matter most, and it has no in-house research team.

Scale

  • 1,136 sides and $1.91B in 2024, No. 153 nationally by volume.3
  • $1.685B in 2025, as the company reports it.4
  • 20 offices, 15 of them on the Neighbor Islands.8
  • About 5.4% of 2025 statewide dollar volume, counting both sides of each sale Derived.

Its business is in the segment that fell

  • Volume went from over $3B in 20216 to $1.685B in 2025.4
  • Its own figures fell 13.5% from 2024 to 2025 ($1.947B → $1.685B), while statewide dollar volume was roughly flat.7,4 The definitions differ, so read the gap as a direction, not an exact number.
  • It is luxury-heavy. It reports half of the state's $30M+ sides from 2014 to 2024.7

A research gap to fill

  • Its Q2 2026 statewide update was written by one agent and partly cites a competitor's reports.12
  • Its Maui Q4 2025 report was compiled from a vendor feed.13
  • We found no research lead or economist on staff.24 Competitor Locations has had in-house research since 1979 and uses it to recruit agents.16,17

Two ways a win spreads

  • The networks it belongs to. Hawaii Life is a founding member of Forbes Global Properties, and its CEO chairs that network's board.5,19 It is also an affiliate of Luxury Portfolio International, part of LeadingRE (500+ firms).18,20 LeadingRE runs pilots with member firms and shares the results across the network.21 The referral-corridor data is exactly what those networks trade on.
  • An appetite for new tools. In March 2026 it named a Director of Agent Success and Innovation who helps develop the company's internal agent platforms.4

Sponsorship without RESPA risk

Lenders and title companies will offer to pay for this. RESPA Section 8 bars anything of value given in exchange for referrals, and "services at special or free rates" count.42,43 The closest precedent: in 2023 the CFPB fined a lender $1.75M and a brokerage $200K over a free data subscription worth about $300 a month given to agents.32

Safe

  • The brokerage pays fair market value itself. This is the default.
  • A title, escrow or lender firm hosts an open "Who's buying Hawaiʻi" seminar using the public edition. It must be open to all agents and the public, carry only the host's brand, and come with no referral strings.44
  • A sponsor that is not a settlement-service provider funds the public edition. Assumption Counsel should confirm.

Only with counsel

  • A settlement provider licenses the public edition at fair market value, under its own brand, for the whole market. It needs a written fair-market-value memo and a log of every item worth more than $5.47
  • Joint advertising with a lender or title firm, where each side pays its share and the split is in writing.48

Never

  • A lender, title or escrow firm pays for any part of a brokerage's license or seats.
  • Access tied to using a particular loan officer or title firm, or to how many referrals are sent.
  • Pricing that scales with referrals, or us taking a share of a referral fee.39

Private RESPA suits can claim three times the charge, and one was filed against Rocket in January 2026.42,51 This is a plan, not legal advice. Get the counsel memo in section 9 before taking any sponsor money.

8

Guardrails built into every product

Fair housing

Label "local, mainland, foreign" as where buyers live, never as nationality or ancestry. No copy that prefers a type of buyer, and no steering. Hawaiʻi law adds protected classes beyond the federal list (HRS §515-3).52 Meta and Google restrict housing ads by age and ZIP code.

Calls and texts

Owner lists carry mailing addresses only. Any phone list needs a Do-Not-Call scrub and a documented exemption. No calls before 8am or after 9pm. We never call or text on a client's behalf.53

Lahaina and Kula

ZIPs 96761, 96767 and 96790 are permanently excluded from every list, offer and prospecting product. The 32nd wildfire proclamation, dated Aug 24, 2026, runs to about Oct 23, 2026. Check whether a 33rd renews the ban on unsolicited offers. Keep the exclusion either way.

Licensing and data

We take no referral pay (HRS 467-14).39 Owner mailing addresses on county rolls are public records (OIP Op. Ltr. 11-01). We publish aggregates only, with no owner names or parcel IDs in reports. No MLS-derived production figures for rival agents.55

Risks and what raises the price

Risks

  • Substitutes. RPR is free, and vendor reports and an in-house build are possible. Sell the analysis and the brokerage-vs-market view, not charts.15
  • One customer. If Hawaii Life wants exclusivity, charge 25–50% more for 12 months on one product line. Assumption
  • Compass consolidation. Compass now owns Coldwell Banker, Corcoran and Sotheby's International Realty, and it is fighting over how agent data is used.55,56
  • Definitions. DBEDT counts deeds, RealTrends counts sides, and brokerages report MLS figures. Label every derived share.

Data upgrades, ranked by effect on price

  • Quarterly Title Guaranty refreshes, now permitted: each new edition updates buyer states, countries and districts.
  • Kauaʻi and Hawaiʻi County owner rolls, which unlock owner lists on all four counties.
  • The client's own closed sales, for the brokerage-vs-market view.
  • Fresh Oʻahu and Maui sales records for every month since Sep 2025.
  • IRS 2023–24 migration data, due November 20, 2026 on the IRS tentative schedule. It is the first year after the Lahaina fire, which makes it a strong reason for a refresh.

Second-wave prospects

  • RE/MAX Hawaii: about 170 agents and 6 offices.57
  • Corcoran Pacific: 100–150 agents and 9 offices, now under Compass.58,56
  • Berkshire Hathaway HomeServices: three separate Hawaiʻi franchises.60
  • Title, escrow and lender firms: they can buy the public edition for their own marketing, within the RESPA rules in section 7.
  • Developers, lenders and appraisers: buyers for custom studies. Locations' research division already sells to this market.17
