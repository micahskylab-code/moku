How to read a TMK

A full TMK like (4) 2-6-003:024 reads left to right: the island code in parentheses (1 Oahu, 2 Maui County, 3 Hawaii Island, 4 Kauai), then zone, section, plat, and parcel. Condo units add a four-digit CPR number on the end. Locals often drop the island digit — Moku understands both forms, with or without dashes.

What you'll see for any parcel

  • Owner of record and parcel acreage, as the county publishes them
  • County zoning, FEMA flood zone, and tsunami evacuation status
  • Public school zones (elementary, middle, high)
  • On Kauai: the county's current tax-year assessed value; on Maui: county-recorded sale prices

Tap any lot on the 3D map, type an address, or paste a TMK — the record card opens with a shareable link you can text to anyone.
