What does the county say your place is worth?

Every Kauai parcel gets a county-assessed value each tax year — the number your property tax is built on. Kauai doesn't make it easy to see. Moku shows the current-year assessment for any parcel, free, straight from the county's own records.

Assessed value is not market value

The assessment is a tax number, set on the county's schedule — it usually sits below what buyers actually pay, sometimes far below. Use it to understand your tax bill and compare neighbors, not to price a sale.

What Moku shows on every Kauai parcel

  • The current tax-year assessed value, from the county's roll
  • Whether a home exemption is on file
  • Tax class, acreage, owner of record, zoning, flood and tsunami status
  • A one-tap link to the county's full official record

Condo buildings are assessed per unit — Moku tells you how many unit records a parcel holds and points you at the county record for the per-unit numbers.
