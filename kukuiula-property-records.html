What you'd pay, and earn

Property tax
$2.59 per $1,000 of assessed value if it's your home (owner-occupied, Kauaʻi FY2026-27). A second home or rental is taxed on a higher tiered schedule ($5.45–$9.40). Moku estimates the exact yearly figure for any parcel.
Long-term rent
~$2,332/mo for a 2-bed, ~$2,866 for a 3-bed — the federal HUD Fair Market Rent benchmark for Kauaʻi.
The market
Kauaʻi's single-family median is about $1.39M (2026 Q1, DBEDT). Look up any Kukuiula home for its exact assessed value and estimated tax.
