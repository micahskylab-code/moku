geodata/ - the real place around Na Hiku, Kukui'ula, south Kaua'i, in the walkthrough's world frame
(feet, y up, our floor = 0; see realsite.json for the anchor, true north and sea level).

Files
  realsite.json        grids, anchor (UTM 4N / lat-lon of our lot), true north, sea level, attribution
  far_h.bin near_h.bin Uint16 height grids (1024 x 1024; far +-55,000 ft, near +-6,000 ft around our lot)
  far.jpg far_2k.jpg   true-colour albedo, far square (4096 / 2048 px, ~8 m per texel)
  near.jpg             true-colour albedo, near square (2048 px, 1.8 m per texel)
  far_w.png near_w.png water masks (roughness / ripples in src/realsite.js)
  features.json        roads (near square), building footprints, trees (world feet)
  community/           the community view's map tiles (src/community.js, docs/community-view.md), a concept
                       visualization: a USDA NAIP aerial photo (Feb 2022) on the land, the rest drawn from open data:
    a_c_r.webp s_c_r.webp   albedo (the matched photo on land), 1 km tiles in UTM 4N (2048 / 512 px)
    n_c_r.webp              lidar ground and crown normals, cast height (lossless)
    w_c_r.png               sea distance (1 step per m, +-127 m), pond water / the photo's weight, sea depth / land cover
    h.bin                   Uint16 ground heights, 5 m, world feet
    community.json          box, tiles, transform, encodings, attribution (one line per source)
    places.json             place pins: names and positions, written by hand from OSM and Overture records

Sources and licences
  Elevation   U.S. Geological Survey 3DEP: 1 m DEM, project HI_FEMAR9Kauai_2019_D19 (lidar 2019-21),
              and 1/3 arc-second seamless DEM tiles n22w160, n23w160. Public domain.
  Imagery     Copernicus Sentinel-2 L2A (ESA), tile 04QDK, 14 scenes Jul 2025 - Oct 2026 and 8 older
              clear scenes (2023-25) for the ground under the modelled lots, via AWS Open Data
              (s3://sentinel-cogs, Element 84). "Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2023-2026."
              Free, full and open (Copernicus data policy), attribution as above.
  Map data    OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL 1.0) and Microsoft ML Building Footprints (ODbL 1.0),
              via Overture Maps Foundation release 2026-09-23.0: roads, ponds and golf water hazards,
              building footprints. features.json and the water masks are derived databases of these
              and are themselves under ODbL 1.0 (attribution: "(c) OpenStreetMap contributors,
              Microsoft").
  Trees       Meta and World Resources Institute, High Resolution Canopy Height Maps (2024), tile
              022211101, CC BY 4.0 (Tolan et al. 2024), modified: resampled, and tree tops and heights
              picked from it.

Community view (geodata/community/), in addition to the sources above
  Aerial photo USDA Farm Service Agency, National Agriculture Imagery Program (NAIP) 2021-2023 Hawai'i, 4-band,
              0.6 m; the box was flown 11 Feb 2022 (quarter quads m_2115904_se, m_2115905_sw, m_2115913_nw).
              Via NOAA Digital Coast, dataset 9668 (https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/inport/item/69406;
              https://coastalimagery.blob.core.windows.net/digitalcoast/HI_NAIP_2021_9668/). "Use Constraints: None.
              The USDA-FSA Aerial Photography Field office asks to be credited in derived products." Public domain.
              Credited as "Aerial photo: USDA NAIP 2022". Modified: reprojected to EPSG:32604 at 0.488 m,
              colour-matched to the Sentinel-2 mosaic, its broad colour taken from it under thin cloud; drawn
              over on the modelled row of lots and in the ponds and sea.
  Elevation   USGS 3DEP 1 m DEM, HI_FEMAR9Kauai_2019_D19, over the box E 447500-453500, N 2418000-2423000
              (UTM 4N), read from s3://prd-tnm. Public domain.
  Seafloor    USACE NCMP 2013 Kaua'i topobathy lidar, bare earth 1 m grid (NOAA Digital Coast dataset 9335,
              s3://noaa-nos-coastal-lidar-pds). Public domain. Sea colour, depth and surf; shore voids.
  Imagery     the Sentinel-2 mosaic above (land cover and tint; the photo's colour match; open-sea colour with the
              near-infrared glint offset taken off). "Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2023-2026."
  Trees       the canopy height tile above (CC BY 4.0, Meta and WRI, Tolan et al. 2024), modified: resampled
              and drawn as crowns, shading and shadows; also used to leave overgrown mapped ponds as land.
  Map data    via Overture Maps Foundation release 2026-09-23.0, all ODbL 1.0: OpenStreetMap contributors
              (roads, paths, ponds, pools, beaches, bare rock, golf courses and features, building
              footprints, place names), TomTom (29 road segments, about 3.7 km, class unknown) and Microsoft
              ML Building Footprints. The tiles' drawn edges, w_*.png and places.json's OSM entries are
              derived from these and are under ODbL 1.0 (https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/);
              attribution "(c) OpenStreetMap contributors, TomTom, Microsoft".
  Places      three pins (The Club, The Lodge, Kukui'ula Small Boat Harbor) are Overture places records
              (confidence >= 0.9), CDLA-Permissive-2.0 (https://cdla.dev/permissive-2-0/). Names are as the
              records give them, with the Hawaiian 'okina restored; nothing is copied from the developer's
              site or map. One OSM name is changed: the beach OSM calls Lawa'i Beach (at Lawa'i Bay) is pinned
              as "Lawa'i Bay beach", to tell it from Lawa'i Beach on Hoai Bay (its note in places.json says so).

Rebuild (python3, numpy scipy pillow rasterio pyproj shapely pyarrow; network to AWS S3)
  python3 scripts/geo_s2_list.py <cache>           # Sentinel-2 scenes over 04QDK
  python3 scripts/geo_s2_score.py <cache>          # cloud over the area from the scene classification
  python3 scripts/geo_s2_mosaic.py                 # cloud-free median mosaic -> <cache>/s2_aoi.npz
  python3 scripts/geo_s2_mosaic.py --before        # older scenes around the strip -> s2_before.npz
  python3 scripts/geo_dem.py                       # lidar + 1/3" DEM -> dem_aoi_10m, dem_near_2m
  python3 scripts/geo_overture.py <cache> transportation/segment   (also base/water base/land_use buildings/building)
  curl -o <dir>/022211101.tif https://dataforgood-fb-data.s3.amazonaws.com/forests/v1/alsgedi_global_v6_float/chm/022211101.tif
  python3 scripts/geo_features.py <dir>/022211101.tif
  GEO_CHM=<dir>/022211101.tif python3 scripts/geo_build.py
  python3 scripts/geo_register.py <overlay.png> 1.66   # registration evidence
  community view (after the steps above):
  python3 scripts/geo_dem1m.py                     # USGS 1 m DEM over the box -> dem_comm_1m.npy
  python3 scripts/geo_bathy.py                     # USACE 2013 topobathy -> bathy_comm_1m.npy
  python3 scripts/geo_overture.py <cache> base/land -159.515 21.86 -159.445 21.915   # beaches, bare rock
  python3 scripts/geo_naip.py                      # NAIP 2022 photo over the box -> naip_comm.npy (tiles in <cache>/../naip)
  GEO_CHM=<dir>/022211101.tif python3 scripts/geo_community.py   # all 30 tiles (~3 min each), h.bin, community.json
  python3 scripts/geo_community.py --only "2,2;1,2"              # some tiles again
  (<cache> defaults to ../geo-test/cache next to the repo; set GEO_CACHE to move it)
