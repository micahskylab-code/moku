County-recorded sales

What Maui homes actually sold for

Sold prices in Hawaii usually hide behind agent logins. Maui County publishes its recorded sales in the public assessment roll — about 1,500 of them, each with a price and date. Moku puts every one on a free map.

What's on the map

Roughly 1,500 county-recorded sales from March 2023 through June 2025, shown as price pills over the satellite map — from Kihei condos to Kapalua estates, plus Molokai and Lanai. Tap any sale to open the full parcel record.

Honest fine print

These are assessment-roll records: they update on the county's cycle and lag the live market by months. They're real recorded transactions — not estimates, not listings — and they're the same records title companies and appraisers pull. Moku just makes them free to see.

Other counties don't publish sales this way yet; when they do, they go on the map the same week.
