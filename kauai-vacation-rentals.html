The county registry, mapped

Is that Kauai vacation rental registered?

Kauai County keeps a public registry of every legally registered non-conforming vacation rental — 602 parcels. It lives in GIS files most people will never open. Moku puts all of them on a free map you can check in ten seconds.

What "non-conforming TVR" means

Outside Kauai's designated visitor areas, a whole-home vacation rental generally needs a registered non-conforming use certificate — grandfathered rentals that operated lawfully before the county's rules tightened. The registry is the county's own list of those parcels.

Who checks this map

  • Neighbors wondering whether the rental down the street is on the list
  • Buyers pricing a property whose income story depends on rental legality
  • Guests checking a booking before they wire a deposit

Every parcel card on Kauai also shows its registry status individually — search any address and look for the "County TVR registry" line.

Registry data is shown exactly as Kauai County publishes it and can change; confirm current status with the county's Planning Department before relying on it.
