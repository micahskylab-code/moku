# Brochure detail audit (fresh-context critics)


## front

Ours is still a long way from the reference. It reads as a clean CG massing study of a subdivision, not a photograph of one house set in jungle above the sea. The biggest gap is the setting and the light together. The reference has a clear deep-blue Kauai sky. The sun is in front of the camera, so the palm fronds glow, shadows fall toward the viewer and the facades sit in soft shade. Behind the house the land drops through dense tropical forest and a golf course with red-dirt paths to a hazy navy ocean. Ours uses a grey South-African cumulus HDRI, lights the scene from behind the camera, and sits the house on a flat green plain lined with cloned white bungalows and 50-ft coconut palms. Saturation is about 28% lower than the reference (mean S 72 vs 99) and the highlights are dull (p95 188 vs 209). The architecture and landscape are also missing the details that sell the reference. It has large louvered Dutch gables, slim same-colour fascias and hips, a cedar carriage garage door with glass lites, narrow louvered shutters set into lap siding, a lit glass entry with a cedar header and ipe landing, a single staggered column of coral-stone pads, lipstick and Christmas palms, plumeria trees, mondo and silver groundcovers, grass-dominant hex pavers and curved concrete mow curbs. Ours has charcoal outlines on every hip and eave, black voids in the glazing, a white double-ladder path, rows of identical crimson ti, and a white lattice driveway. It is also rendered at 640x400 with visible denoise smear.
