What you'd pay, and earn

Property tax
$3.50 per $1,000 of assessed value if it's your home (owner-occupied, Oʻahu FY2026-27). A second home or rental is taxed on a higher tiered schedule (up to $11.40). Moku estimates the exact yearly figure for any parcel.
Long-term rent
~$2,642/mo for a 2-bed, ~$3,674 for a 3-bed — the federal HUD Fair Market Rent benchmark for Oʻahu.
The market
Oʻahu's single-family median is about $1.18M (2026 Q1, DBEDT). Look up any Kaneohe home for its exact assessed value and estimated tax.
